DRIVER profile: driverId, organizationId, batchId, cabId, batchName, cabRegNumber
COMMUTER profile: commuterId, organizationId, batchId, popId, cabId, isComing, busPassNo, validFrom, validTo, batchName, popName
STAFF profile: COMMUTER + designation, employeeType
